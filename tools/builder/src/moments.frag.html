Recommendation Prompts

Decision Moments

One base persona, in one buyer situation, at the moment they are ready to act. Describe the moment, agree the one prompt that person would type, then turn it into 10 recommendation prompts that one piece of content should answer.

Input

Base Personas

The saved persona set this moment comes from. Its ICP supplies the buyer situations and the alternatives.

In The Night Ventures

Decision Moments

Moments already pushed for the company chosen at the top of the page. Open one to change it and push again.

Step A

The Moment

Who the person is and the moment they are in, in your own words: their position, their constraints, what just happened and what they now want to do.

Step B

Seed Prompt

The one prompt this person would type to an AI at that moment. It asks who or what to use. The note under it says how the answer should lead to the company; your brand never appears in the prompt.

Earlier versions (0)

Step C

Prompt Set

Recommendation prompts from the seed, 10 unless you choose another number: the same person, the same moment, spread evenly over five angles. Each is checked for duplicates (within the set and against the company's prompts in The Night Ventures), for being a recommendation prompt, for staying in the moment and for not naming your brand.

Step D

Push and Test

Writes the moment and its prompts to The Night Ventures, as Approved prompts linked to the moment. Then run them in Prompt Tests.
